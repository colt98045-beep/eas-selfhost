Scheduled maintenance

Plan a lockout ahead of time. The site locks at the start and unlocks at the end on its own (checked every 5 minutes), and an optional maintenance announcement shows from now until the window ends. The manual switch above always wins. Windows can be up to 7 days long.

Incidents

Health-check outages, lockouts and manual incidents from the last 30 days. Post timestamped updates (shown on /status and the launcher banner); posting Resolved closes an incident. A new incident here (security, outage, degraded performance and so on) never locks a site or changes its up/down status. You can edit or delete only your own updates.
